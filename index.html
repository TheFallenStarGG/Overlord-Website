What you get

  • Economy that sticks around

    Work, fish, mine, dailies, shop, bank, stocks, and games when your server wants them. Balances are per server so each community has its own market.

  • World Campaign

    Every server is one house on a shared map. Recruit, march, siege cities, and fight for provinces over a season. Live board on the Campaign page.

  • The Gazette

    A daily newspaper-style image for the realm—not a wall of embeds. Admins can route it to an events channel; anyone can browse Gazette.

  • Moderation

    Warn, mute, kick, ban, purge, slowmode, lock, roles, automod, anti-raid, and welcome messages. Prefix-only and listed under !!modhelp.

  • Raids & world noise

    Cross-server raids, events, bosses, and realm boards when you want the map to feel busy. Admins can tune or quiet pieces with !!settings.

  • Built for Discord

    Commands use !!. !!help is the in-Discord index; this site is for maps, stocks, status, and reading at a desk.

First five minutes

  1. Add the bot (button above, or !!invite from a server that already has it).
  2. Run !!help — that’s the table of contents.
  3. Try !!daily, !!work, or !!balance.
  4. Admins: !!settings, !!events-channel, !!modhelp.
  5. Optional: vote on top.gg, then !!vote.
